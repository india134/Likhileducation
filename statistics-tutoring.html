Descriptive statistics

Questions

Statistics tutoring: common questions

Do you follow my university's statistics syllabus?

Yes. Quantelite builds each plan from the student's own course outline, lecture notes, assignments and past exams, because statistics courses vary widely between universities.

Do you teach probability as well as statistics?

Yes. Probability, random variables and distributions are taught alongside estimation, hypothesis testing and regression.

Can you help with statistical software?

Sessions focus on the statistical reasoning. Where a course uses a tool such as Python, R or Excel, we can work through examples that use it.

Are sessions available in my time zone?

Sessions are scheduled across North American, UK and European, Middle East and Asia-Pacific time zones. Every session is live, 1:1 and online.
