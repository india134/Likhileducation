Curriculum

Calculus topics we teach

Coverage is customised to your university's syllabus. These are the areas students most often bring to us.

01

Limits & continuity

Epsilon-delta reasoning where required, and the limit laws behind every derivative.

02

Differentiation & applications

Rules, implicit differentiation, optimization, related rates and curve analysis.

03

Integration techniques

Substitution, integration by parts, partial fractions, trig substitution and improper integrals.

04

Sequences & series

Convergence tests, power series, and Taylor and Maclaurin expansions.

05

Multivariable calculus

Partial derivatives, gradients, Lagrange multipliers, double and triple integrals.

06

Vector calculus

Line and surface integrals; Green's, Stokes' and divergence theorems.

07

Differential equations

First- and second-order ODEs and their applications, where your course includes them.

Questions

Calculus tutoring: common questions

Do you follow my university's calculus syllabus?

Yes. University calculus courses vary widely, so Quantelite builds each plan from the student's own course outline, lecture notes, assignments and past exams.

Which calculus courses do you tutor?

Single-variable calculus (Calculus I and II), multivariable calculus and vector calculus, and introductory differential equations where they are part of the course.

Can you help with my assignments?

Sessions focus on understanding the methods behind your problem sets so you can complete them yourself. We do not complete graded work on a student's behalf.

Are sessions available in my time zone?

Sessions are scheduled across North American, UK and European, Middle East and Asia-Pacific time zones. Every session is live, 1:1 and online.
