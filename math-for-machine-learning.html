Curriculum

What we cover

Coverage is tailored to your background, and to your course syllabus where you are taking one.

01

Vectors & vector spaces

Span, linear independence, basis and dimension: the language of data.

02

Matrices as transformations

Matrix multiplication, rank, inverses and determinants, understood geometrically.

03

Eigenvalues & decompositions

Eigenvectors, diagonalization, SVD and their role in PCA and dimensionality reduction.

04

Orthogonality & least squares

Projections, orthonormal bases and the geometry of linear regression.

05

Multivariable calculus

Partial derivatives, gradients, Jacobians and the chain rule behind backpropagation.

06

Optimization & gradient descent

Convexity, loss functions, learning rates and why gradient descent converges.

07

Probability for ML

Random variables, distributions, Bayes' theorem, expectation and likelihood.

08

Statistics for ML

Estimation, maximum likelihood, bias and variance, and model evaluation.

Questions

Math for Machine Learning tutoring: common questions

What mathematics do I need for machine learning?

The core areas are linear algebra (vectors, matrices, eigenvalues, SVD), multivariable calculus (gradients and the chain rule), optimization (gradient descent) and probability and statistics (distributions, likelihood, estimation).

Do you tutor university linear algebra courses?

Yes. Quantelite tutors university linear algebra, customised to the student's own syllabus, including proof-based courses.

Is this a coding or AI engineering course?

No. This program focuses on mathematics. Short Python examples are used where they help illustrate an idea, but the goal is mathematical understanding.

Who teaches this program?

Akash Gupta, founder of Quantelite, who holds a B.Tech from IIT (BHU) and a Master's in Artificial Intelligence from IIT Kharagpur.

Are sessions available in my time zone?

Sessions are scheduled across North American, UK and European, Middle East and Asia-Pacific time zones. Every session is live, 1:1 and online.
