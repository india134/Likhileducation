Curriculum

What we cover in AP Precalculus

Sessions follow the College Board course framework. Units 1 to 3 are assessed on the AP exam; Unit 4 is taught for schools and students who cover it.

01

Polynomial & rational functions

Rates of change, zeros, end behaviour, asymptotes and transformations of functions.

02

Exponential & logarithmic functions

Arithmetic and geometric sequences, exponential models, inverses, logarithms and semi-log plots.

03

Trigonometric & polar functions

The unit circle, sinusoidal models, inverse trig functions, identities and polar graphs.

04

Parameters, vectors & matrices

Parametric functions, vectors, matrices as transformations and implicit functions (Unit 4).

05

Modeling with functions

Choosing, building and validating function models from data and context.

06

Exam skills

Calculator and non-calculator multiple choice, and clear written free-response answers.

Questions

AP Precalculus tutoring: common questions

What is AP Precalculus?

AP Precalculus is a College Board course that studies polynomial, rational, exponential, logarithmic, trigonometric and polar functions, and how they model real situations. It prepares students for AP Calculus and other college-level mathematics.

Is AP Precalculus required before AP Calculus?

It is not formally required, but it builds exactly the function skills that AP Calculus depends on. Students who take it usually find AP Calculus noticeably easier.

Which units are on the AP Precalculus exam?

Units 1 to 3 (polynomial and rational functions; exponential and logarithmic functions; trigonometric and polar functions) are assessed on the exam. Unit 4 is optional and not assessed.

Are sessions available in my time zone?

Sessions are scheduled across North American, UK and European, Middle East and Asia-Pacific time zones. Every session is live, 1:1 and online.
