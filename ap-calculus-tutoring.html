Curriculum

What we cover in AP Calculus

Sessions follow the College Board course framework. AB covers Units 1 to 8; BC covers all ten units.

01

Limits & continuity

Limits graphically, numerically and algebraically; continuity and the Intermediate Value Theorem.

02

Differentiation

Definition of the derivative, rules, the chain rule, implicit and inverse-function differentiation.

03

Contextual applications

Related rates, linearization, motion along a line and L'Hospital's rule.

04

Analytical applications

Mean Value Theorem, extrema, concavity, curve sketching and optimization.

05

Integration

Riemann sums, the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, antiderivatives and integration techniques.

06

Differential equations

Slope fields, separation of variables, exponential models; Euler's method and logistic growth for BC.

07

Applications of integration

Average value, accumulation, area between curves and volumes of solids.

08

BC: parametric, polar & vectors

Calculus of parametric, polar and vector-valued functions.

09

BC: infinite series

Convergence tests, Taylor and Maclaurin series, error bounds and radius of convergence.

Questions

AP Calculus tutoring: common questions

What is the difference between AP Calculus AB and BC?

AP Calculus AB covers limits, derivatives, integrals and their applications (Units 1 to 8). AP Calculus BC covers everything in AB plus parametric, polar and vector-valued functions and infinite series (Units 9 and 10). BC students also receive an AB subscore.

Do you tutor both AB and BC?

Yes. Quantelite tutors both AP Calculus AB and AP Calculus BC, 1:1 and online, and adapts each plan to the student's course, school pace and exam date.

How do you prepare students for the free-response section?

Students practise released free-response questions under timed conditions, and their answers are reviewed against the official scoring guidelines, with focus on justification, notation and units.

When should my child start AP Calculus tutoring?

Ideally at the start of the school year, so concepts are secure before each unit test. Students can also join mid-year or for focused exam preparation from February onwards.

Are sessions available in my time zone?

Sessions are scheduled across North American, UK and European, Middle East and Asia-Pacific time zones. Every session is live, 1:1 and online.
