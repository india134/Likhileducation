The Quantelite philosophy

Understanding is the shortcut. There is no other.

Most tutoring teaches students to recognise problems. We teach them to reason through problems they have never seen before. That's the skill that moves an exam score from a 4 to a 5, and it's the same skill that machine learning, finance and research careers are built on.

i.

Concept before procedure

Every formula is derived, visualised and questioned before it's practised, so it stays understood instead of memorised and forgotten.

ii.

Rigour with warmth

High standards and real patience. Students are pushed hard, and they are never made to feel small for not knowing yet.

iii.

Built for what's next

Exams matter, and we prepare for them precisely. The deeper goal is a mind ready for university mathematics, data and AI.

What is Quantelite?

Quantelite is a premium, private online tutoring practice offering personalized 1:1 instruction in AP Calculus, AP Precalculus, AP Statistics, IB Mathematics, university calculus, statistics and linear algebra, and the mathematical foundations of AI and machine learning. It is led by IIT-trained mentor Akash Gupta.

What subjects does Quantelite teach?

Quantelite offers 1:1 online tutoring in AP Calculus AB and BC, AP Precalculus, AP Statistics, IB Mathematics (Analysis & Approaches and Applications & Interpretation, at SL and HL), university-level calculus, statistics and linear algebra, mathematics for machine learning, and a skills-based AI & Machine Learning program for students aged roughly 10 to 18.

Is the AI & ML program tied to a school curriculum?

No. It is independent of any school board. It builds real, career-ready skills: computational thinking, Python, data literacy, the mathematics behind machine learning, and hands-on projects students can show in portfolios and university applications.

How are sessions delivered, and which time zones do you cover?

Every session is live, 1:1 and online, with a shared digital whiteboard, curated problem sets and regular progress reports for parents. Sessions are scheduled across North American, UK and European, Middle East and Asia-Pacific time zones.

Who will teach my child?

Sessions are led by founder Akash Gupta, a B.Tech graduate of IIT (BHU) with a Master's in Artificial Intelligence from IIT Kharagpur, who has mentored 200+ students across 6+ countries.

What results do students achieve?

Quantelite students have earned 5s and 4s on AP Calculus BC, AP Calculus AB, AP Precalculus and AP Statistics. Just as important, they leave with lasting understanding and confidence.
